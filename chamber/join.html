NP Membership

NP Membership is available to nonprofit organizations that contribute to the local community.

  • Free membership
  • Access to chamber events
  • Community networking opportunities
  • Training opportunities
  • Community event participation

Bronze Membership

Bronze Membership provides essential networking and promotional opportunities for small businesses.

  • All NP membership benefits
  • Business networking events
  • Member discounts
  • Basic business training
  • Social media recognition

Silver Membership

Silver Membership gives established businesses additional opportunities to promote their services.

  • All Bronze membership benefits
  • Additional event discounts
  • Priority networking opportunities
  • Business training sessions
  • Website business spotlight opportunities

Gold Membership

Gold Membership provides the highest level of promotional and networking opportunities.

  • All Silver membership benefits
  • Premium business promotion
  • Priority event registration
  • Featured business spotlight opportunities
  • Additional advertising opportunities
  • Exclusive networking events
